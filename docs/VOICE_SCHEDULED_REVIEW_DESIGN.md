# Voice entry to due-action review

Support review due action (one due candidate), review next due action (earliest dueAt, stable ID order for ties), and review scheduled action UUID. This selects a plan for presentation, never grants execution. Saved actions in due pending/blocked/waiting-conditions/awaiting-review states are candidates; scheduler rechecks actual due time, conditions, target and occurrence under its mutex. Foreground explicit review may promote a due action to AWAITING_REVIEW without depending on worker timing or notification access. It does not run the action.

UI and voice use the same review helper. Preserve the initiating command source and original scheduled registry through internet orchestration. Scheduled registries require exact confirmation even when normal policy allows an action immediately; HIGH risk still requires Android authentication first. This requirement is constructor policy outside serialized actions and persists across deferred connectivity. Existing confirmation deadlines, one-use voice gates and due-occurrence/target/condition/cancellation guards remain unchanged.

Tests must establish no effect merely from review, force-confirm source/expiry/replay behavior, deterministic selection and future/non-action exclusion, actual persisted due review through ViewModel, cancellation after review and successful confirmed low-risk execution. Injected verified grants do not prove acoustic identity or actual owner approval. Multi-task voice list selection beyond chronological next/explicit IDs and physical acceptance remain unfinished.
