# Voice deletion of personal facts

`forget my <subject>` and `forget what I told you about my <subject>` resolve only explicit semantic personal-fact records with that exact normalized subject. Prepare a destructive typed action describing current values, total record count and inclusion of historical records. Bind it to a SHA256 snapshot of all candidate record IDs/content/status/source/timestamps; the hash is not authentication. Require normal Android authentication followed by fresh exact confirmation. Refuse mixed plans, schedules and taught routines. No effect on preparation/authentication alone.

At execution, under privacy mutex and Room transaction, reconstruct the exact action from current plus historical/expired records, compare it to the reviewed snapshot, delete every matching record and FTS entry, and verify no matches remain. Any new/corrected/deleted fact or metadata change refuses without deleting. Other subjects and inferred/structured records are outside this command. Private mode blocks learning, not an explicitly authorized deletion. Audit remains metadata-only. Transient review/conversation may display the requested values; this does not promise secure erasure of storage pages/backups or deleting all related conversational text.

Tests: strict parsing/validation/codec/risk, authentication-confirmation ordering and no effects before approval, real encrypted storage changed-target refusal, history/current deletion, related-subject preservation, private-mode deletion and absence from local search. Physical credential/voice acceptance remains separate.
